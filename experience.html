The Bridal Trial Experience (Booked Brides Only)

Every artist on my team is handpicked and personally trained by me to ensure they can execute the same level of artistry and signature style that I am known for. Each team artist works alongside me, assisting bridal parties for 1-2 years before advancing to working with brides. During this time, they undergo multiple training sessions with me and other highly skilled artists to refine their techniques and ensure their skills and customer service align perfectly with my vision and brand. My goal is not only to maintain flawless artistry but also to provide an elevated, luxury experience for every bride. By the time a team artist begins working with brides, they have developed the same level of expertise and precision as I have, ensuring they can execute any of my signature looks with confidence and perfection.
